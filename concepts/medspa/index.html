The day

Two ways to spend a day.

  1. For quiet

    The Still Day

    Guided meditation in rooms built for silence, a slow facial, warm oil worked deep into the muscles. You leave lighter than you arrived.

  2. For energy

    The Awake Day

    A mineral salt scrub, contrast bathing and a sculpting facial that brings colour back to the skin. You leave lit from within.

  3. Either way

    The thermal suite

    Cedar sauna, eucalyptus steam and red-light therapy, open to you all day. Move between them at your own pace.

Nothing is decided until you arrive.
