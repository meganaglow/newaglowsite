The day

Two ways to spend a day.

  1. For quiet

    The Still Day

    Guided meditation in rooms built for silence, a slow facial, and warm oil worked deep into tired shoulders. You leave lighter than you arrived.

  2. For energy

    The Awake Day

    A mineral salt scrub, warm and cool pools, and a lifting facial that brings colour back to the skin. You leave lit from within.

  3. Either way

    The thermal suite

    Cedar sauna, eucalyptus steam and red-light therapy, open to you all day, with quiet loungers in between. Nothing is timed. Stay as long as you like.

Nothing is decided until you arrive.
