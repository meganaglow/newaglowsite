Packages

Custom packages, shaped around your business

Aglow focuses on the website itself: the design, structure, content experience and practical tools that help a client-facing business run more smoothly online. Every engagement is quoted after we talk, because a one-page refresh and a full rebuild are not the same job.

  • Custom website design

    Best for a new site or full rethink

    A ground-up website built around your business rather than forced into a generic template. Includes site structure, visual design, responsive development, content shaping and launch support.

  • Website refresh

    Best when the bones are still good

    A focused redesign for an existing site that feels dated, cluttered, inconsistent or harder to use than it should be. We keep what is working and improve what is not.

  • Smart site setup

    Practical, not flashy

    Booking, inquiry forms, email capture, scheduling links, FAQs and light automation. AI can be part of the toolkit where it genuinely reduces work, but it is never the point of the website.

  • Ongoing website care

    Optional after launch

    For owners who would rather send a note than log into their website. Updates, small changes and routine upkeep can be handled without turning you into your own webmaster.

  • Back-office AI setup

    Add-on

    Setting up Anthropic’s Claude for Small Business inside the tools you already use, configuring your first few workflows, and training you on them in plain English. Requires your own Claude subscription, billed by Anthropic rather than by me.
